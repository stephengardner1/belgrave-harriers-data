National Junior Athletics League Results 2006: Match 4, Brent Division Division, Hemel Hempstead

2006-08-13

There was a shortage of competitors at this fixture, with no takers at all for the sprint hurdles, probably due to a number of factors including a young athletes’ SEAA Combined Events championships over the weekend. No shortage of rain showers though and the officials lost count of the number of times they changed in and out of rain gear – at least the thunder, rumbling for half the afternoon, kept out of our way. The Sutton sprinters dominated the sprints and won the 4x100m relay, Idowu Akindele having an especially good day and winning the Athlete of the Match award.

Men. 100m: 1 T Gilling U17 11.7; 1 I Akindele 11.6. 200m: 1 I Akindele 23.0; 2 B Osonaike U17 25.8. 400m: 2 M Kirby U17 53.6; 3 B Osonaike U17 56.8. LJ: 4 I Akindele 5.35. SP: 3 T Gilling U17 9.94; 2 M Kirby U17 7.81. 4x100m: 1 Belgrave/Sutton 45.8 (B Osonaike U17, T Gilling U17, M Kirby U17, I Akindele).

Women. 100m: 1 C Cole U17 13.0; 1 E Barnard U17 13.9. 200m: 1 C Cole U17 27.5. 1 A Chung U17 29.1. 400m: 1 C Cole U17 60.9; 1 A Chung U17 69.3. 800m: 2 C Chambers 2:49.6. LJ: 1 E Barnard U17 4.89; 2 A Chung U17 3.71. SP: 3 C Chambers 5.59. 4x100m: 1 Belgrave/Sutton 54.2 (E Barnard U17, A Chung U17, C Chambers, C Cole U17).

Teams. 1 Vale of Aylesbury 383; 2 Thames Valley H 294; 3 Wycombe Phoenix 253; 4 Dacorum & Tring 223; 5 Belgrave/Sutton & Dist 217; 6 Milton Keynes AC 79; 7 Guildford & Godalming AC 58.
