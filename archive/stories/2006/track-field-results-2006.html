IAAF World Junior Champs, Beijing, 15-16 August 2006. a=15, b=16

Wade Bennett-Jackson set the fastest heat time of 10.33 - and it ended up fastest of the whole competition - but he could only manage 6th in a final where a blanket finish had only 5/100ths covered positions 3rd to 7th. Having led the field at 60m there was an agonising wait for Wade to see who actually got the bronze. But up front Harry Aikines-Ayreetey was the winner, adding the U20 global title to the 100m/200m he won in the World Youth Championships last year.

The second half of Britain's sprint relay squad was taken control of by Belgrave and Sutton sprinters. Wade and Harry teamed up to third place in 39.24, 3/100ths behind the USA. Jamaica ran out winners in 39.05.

European Championships, Gothenburg, Day 4, 10 August 2006

In qualifying group A of the triple jump, Phillips Idowu sailed through to the final with a second effort of 17.01 metres. Not so lucky was Frenchman Julien Kapek, in group B, whose best of 16.74 metres came with his third and last jump. This placed him 13th overall with the 12th and last qualifying place having been bagged at 16.75! What a disappointment for the man who recently won the French Championship with 17.38.

Also in group B was the favourite Christian Olsson of Sweden; his very first effort took him out to 17.51 metres - best of the day by a long way.

Men. TJ: 3 Qual A P Idowu 17.01 (16.44/1.5, 17.01, -1.0); 7 Qual B J Kapek 16.74 (16.35/1.0, 16.04/1.0, 16.74/0.4).

European Championships, Gothenburg, Day 3, 9 August 2006

With nowhere near enough preparation due to a groin injury that delayed his start to the season, Tim Benjamin did well to place 6th in the final of the 400m. He fought well in the home straight and was once again not so far from his season's best - but on this occasion he was nowhere near the form required to tackle France's Marc Raquil.

A nervous looking Ryan Dinham faced up to his first major international event in heat one of the 400 metre hurdles but was hit by a dq - presumably for a trailing leg violation. Drawn in the outside lane, the early part of his race was fine but he tightened when overtaken by the race favourites just inside him and made a mess of hurdle six while changing his stride pattern.

Men. 400m: 6 T Benjamin 45.89. 400mH: dq h1 R Dinham.
