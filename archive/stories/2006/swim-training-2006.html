Swim training

2006-12-07

Swim training for triathletes, injured runners ... in fact anyone anyone who fancies some alternative exercise. A swim coach is usually present if you want to brush up on your technique.

Training takes place on Thursday nights at 9pm in a private pool (Kings College School) in Wimbledon - just for our use.

The club has block booked ten sessions - and we hope to extend this - but we are looking for an average of 15 swimmers per session to break even. Cost is £4 per session which includes coaching.

All are welcome but please Contact us to let us know you are coming and so we can give you further details of location.
