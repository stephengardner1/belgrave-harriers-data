National Junior Athletics League Results 2006: Match 2, Brent Division Division, Perivale

2006-06-11

The previous day's county schools championships did no teams any favours and turn-out all round was lower than in match one. Nevertheless, the Belgrave/Sutton women's contingent held up well and made up for the lower number of boys. As a result we placed second again with all teams occupying the same positions as in the first match at Guildford.
