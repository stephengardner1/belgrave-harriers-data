National Junior Athletics League Results 2006: Match 1, Brent Division Division, Guildford

Agreat start to the season saw our Under 20s run out second in the match and the only reason that they lost was because there were quite a few events not covered. This is the first season that the men's and women's events have been combined to produce an overall team score and the signs are that the Belgrave/Sutton partnership is going to work well.
