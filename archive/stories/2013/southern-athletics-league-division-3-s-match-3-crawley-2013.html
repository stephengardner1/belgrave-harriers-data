Southern Athletics League Division 3 (S), Match 3, Crawley

2013-06-15

A massive win at K2

The boys and girls of the Southern Athletic League racked up a huge score at Crawley and were so intent on a charge up the league table that shorter distance runners stepped up to fill the almost always vacant steeplechase slots to keep the score climbing. We reached 319 points in the end and moved a little closer to the promotion zone - now 5th in the league.
