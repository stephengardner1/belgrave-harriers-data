Our Trainers offer 1-1 or group training and they have expertise in the following areas:

Recreational running up to marathon, gym and general fitness, post & pre natal, biomechanical screening, nutrition & weight management, circuits, boxing, kickboxing, sports massage & rehab.

They can help you gain fitness for a variety of sports such as racquet sport, football, rugby, hockey, cycling and triathlon.

All of our trainers are REPS qualified to at least level 3 and are also qualified UKA Athletics coaches.
