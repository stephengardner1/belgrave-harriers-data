New opportunities within Belgrave Harriers

2005-03-06

Athletics is at an exciting cross roads. Opportunities lie ahead for individuals willing and able to contribute to the development of our sport with the help of this great Club. Our aim is to strengthen management teams and enhance the future provision of services to athletes and the community.

We offer training and development opportunities for:-

Team management posts

Community liaison managers

Schools link development managers

Coaches

If you are keen to get involved and help develop new revenue and project streams then contact Belgrave Harriers initially by email. We have the right track record to help you to help others.

Additionally we welcome individuals who would like to get involved but can only commit a limited amount of time.
