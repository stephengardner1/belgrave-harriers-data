2XU BELGRAVE BOLT 5k - 9 August, Wimbledon (Run under UKA Rules, SEAA permit applied for)

Results: Individuals Teams Results archive (including 2008 Bolt results)

Hope your run went well ... very many thanks to all who helped ... and especially to our race sponsors 2XU.

The Open 2XU BELGRAVE BOLT 5k Road Race incorporating the Surrey County 5k Championship and the final race in the 2009 Surrey Road League series will be run on Sunday 9 August starting at 10am in Cottenham Park Road, Wimbledon.

Two lap course with kilometre markers.

“Closing” date 3rd August (or earlier if 400 entries received – check website for latest information).

We will post your race number to you (no SAE required) but your number must be collected on the day if your entry is received after 3rd August.

Changing, toilets, registration, presentation and refreshments at:
Belgrave Hall, Denmark Road, off Ridgway, Wimbledon (ten minutes jog from the start).

Individual prizes in the Open Race will be presented by for both men and women to the value of: 1st – £40, 2nd – £20, 3rd – £10.

First team prizes will be presented by for both men’s and women’s teams in the Open Race to the value of £30.

Surrey County medals will be awarded for the first three individual places in the Surrey County A.A. Championship for Senior Men and Senior Women. Surrey County medals will also be awarded for first place only to Surrey County A.A. qualified veteran runners in the following age categories M40, M50, M60, W35, W45, W55. For Surrey qualification rules – see * below.

Surrey County team medals will be awarded for the first three men’s and first three women’s teams in the Surrey County A.A. Championship. For Surrey qualification rules – see * below.

In both the Open Team race and Surrey County Team race the result will be calculated using finish positions of the first three eligible runners from each club. Team entry is not necessary.

Entry fee for each runner – £5 (£7 if not registered with England Athletics) or £7 (£9 if not registered with England Athletics) on the day if race limit has not been reached. Cheques must be made payable to Belgrave Harriers.
Send entries to The Event Secretary, 29 Kingston Road, Ewell, Epsom, Surrey KT17 2EG.

If planning to enter on the day, check the website first to ensure that further entries will be allowed.

Email for enquiries – info@belgraveharriers.com.
