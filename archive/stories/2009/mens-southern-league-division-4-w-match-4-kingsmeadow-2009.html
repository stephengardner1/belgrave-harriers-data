Men's Southern League Division 4 (W) Match 4, Kingsmeadow

2009-07-11

Four wins out of four

Momentum was maintained, in fact increased, in this fourth Southern League match of the season. No fewer than 17 names made the team sheet - compare that with the five or six we usually managed last year. The result? Another team win ... and with just one more meeting to go, at Sutton, the chance of going through the season unbeaten.

Welcome to new man Hugo Hattingh who cleared 1.90m in the high jump - but has a 2.07m on his CV from some six or seven years ago. Our four throwers were, as usual, devastating; they shared the events around, odd men out taking a non-scoring role, and came up with a nigh on perfect score of seven wins.

Richard Ward boosted the middle-distance events and took a good scalp over 1500m - that of Tom Carter, now of London AC, who has run 3:57 for the mile and 3:39 for 1500m - how's that for division four competition?
