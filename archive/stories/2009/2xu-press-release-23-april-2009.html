Belgrave Harriers Race Faster and Recover Quicker with 2XU Compression

2XU from Melbourne, Australia have added Belgrave Harriers to its impressive list of sporting institutions that support the brand; The Australian Institute of Sport, The New Zealand Academy of Sport and Athletics Australia to name a few. Mike Trees UK distributor of 2XU said “ with such a depth of high quality Institutions and clubs coming on board, 2XU compression will soon be a house hold name amongst British athletes. Our new generation of 70 denier fabric with 15% more support and 25% more power, is really helping convert runners to 2XU compression.”
