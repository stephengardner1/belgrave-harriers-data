February 2002

23-24

AAA U20, U17 & U15 Champs., Birmingham.

Having taken the Senior AAA 200 metre title while still a 16 year-old and ignoring the possibility of qualifying for the European Indoor Champs. in Vienna, Amy Spencer reverted to competing in her own age-group for the AAA U17 Championships at Birmingham and as a result smashed the 300 metre Championship Best Performance by over 2 seconds. The heats saw her make the record her own, her manually timed 39.7 reducing the previous best of 39.84. By the semi-final stage she was down to 38.93 - and another whole second and more came off in the final where her 37.72 moved her into second place on the UK all-time list for Seniors.

James Ellington was a double winner in the U17 men's sprints. He ran 6.93 in semi-final and final to take the 60m and become the UK's =3rd fastest ever for his age but went one better in the 200m where his winning 21.80 has only ever been beaten by Cardiff's Tim Benjamin.

Silver medals went to Onen Eyong (U20 LJ), Chris Gearing (U17 Shot), Graeme Oudney (U20 800m) and Laurence Oboh (U20 60m).

Lansing Linde Severnside Trophy, Cardiff. Four more Belgrave indoor records fell in this England vs Wales vs Athletics Ireland vs Europen Select match at Cardiff. Another great 800 metre run from Emma Ward found her less than a second short of the European Championships qualifying standard as she was narrowly beaten by Euro Select runner Judit Varga. 10

BMC Races, Cardiff. Stephen Sharp even surprised himself by winning this BMC 1500m race in only his second indoor run of the winter. It was set up to help Cardiff’s James Thie beat the World Indoor qualifying standard and sure enough the Welshman moved ahead after an opening 1:59, followed closely by Steve. The required pace began to slip away over the second half and with Thie tiring it was the Belgrave man who drew into the lead with 50m remaining. It wasn’t far from a family double either, as Maria Sharp chased hard to gain second in the women’s event, just slipping over 4:30 pace towards the conclusion of the race.

Men. 1500m: 1 S.Sharp 3:47.78. Women. 1500m: 2 M.Sharp 4:34.25.

AAA Champs., Cardiff. a=2, b=3.

Ahugely successful Indoor Champs., from a Belgrave point of view, was crowned by Amy Spencer’s scintillating win over 200 metres where she beat Sarah Reilly (our own Brendan’s wife) to become a AAA Champion at the age of 16! Club records were set across the board as Emma Ward, Sarah Claxton and Rachel King all gained silver medals. David Anderson, on his way back from a niggling foot injury, added to the medal tally with a hard fought run over 3000 metres whilst Ben Challenger showed signs that he is returning to good form with his high jump win. Paul Williamson took third spot in the pole vault.
