CAU Inter-Counties, Bedford.

Belgrave throwers made a big impact at the Inter-Counties Championships. Shelley Newman sent her discus winging out to 56.18 to win the event for Surrey, while representing Northern Ireland, Eva Massey beat the qualifying distance for the Commonwealth Games by placing 3rd in the shot with a club record breaking 15.37m. In the colours of Cambridgeshire 19 year old Goldie Sayers won the javelin with a huge throw of 52.94m. Goldie was far from being the youngest winner, however. Not yet 17, Amy Spencer became the youngest Inter Counties winner of all time, easily taking the 200 metres with a superb 23.64. Other medallists were Jennifer Culley in the 400m hurdles and Emma Davies in the 800m.

There were no winners among the men – not surprising when our team was competing in the European Champion Clubs Cup in Lisbon – but Stephen Sharp registered a nifty 2nd in the 5000m and Dominic Girdler also placed 2nd in the sprint hurdles.
