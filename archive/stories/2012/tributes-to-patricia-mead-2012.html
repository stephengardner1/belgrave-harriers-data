Tributes to Patricia Mead

2012-09-08

Patricia Anne Mead 1945-2010

" ... I was greatly shocked and upset. Pat was a lovely lady, loved by all, you could not have had a nicer person we will all have very fond memories of her. " ... when I first stepped inside the Belgrave tent how nervous I was as it was full of proper (!) runners and I thought wouldn't be anything like the small local running club I'd come from. How very wrong I was, everyone was welcoming .... [She] always remembered everything about everyone and always had time to ask how you were and how something was going about something you'd told her about weeks ago. She always made sure you had a piece of cake and any family member that you'd brought in with you had a cake and cuppa too. There was never a shortage of cake and tea and a slice and cup was always found who ever you were. If ever you left kit behind it would always be returned at your next meeting ... freshly washed and always smelt so fresh. We used to tease her about how much fabric conditioner she must have got through. ... "
