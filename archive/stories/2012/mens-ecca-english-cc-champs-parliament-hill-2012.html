Men's ECCA English CC Champs., Parliament Hill

2012-02-25

A new name on the 'Parker Bowl'

Alan Mead writes:- One hundred and twenty five National Cross Country races have now taken place and this latest edition, held on a balmy spring-like day, was a classic as the perhaps overlooked Keith Gerrard of Newham & Essex Beagles proved unbeatable.

It wasn’t a bad day for the Bels as we turned out ten men and reminded ourselves that tucked away among our non-runners was an entered sextet that could have given team winners Leeds City a run for their money had they been in good 'nick' or otherwise available. Still, it’s not about those one enters but those who actually run and we had to settle for a final team spot of 25th. It’s been far worse than that.

Yet another super run came from James Kelly (who, nevertheless, was hoping for something a little higher) and thus a new name goes onto the ‘Parker Bowl’ awarded to the first Belgravian to finish. Good to see Tom Hadfield and Kevin Gadd turning out – their first outings this winter!

Always enjoying his racing, Malik Nehaily was pleased to hear his name being called out by our men’s team as they paused in their preparations to give him a shout as he passed the flag.
