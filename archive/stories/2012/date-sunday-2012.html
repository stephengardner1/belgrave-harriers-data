Officiating in athletics - courses

There are still lots of places available for the Level 2 Field, Track and Marksman courses at Sutton Arena are all now posted on the England Athletics website. A recent development also allows anyone over the age of 14 to apply for the course as opposed to 16 yrs of age before, details as follows:-

Venue : Sutton Arena Leisure Centre, Middleton Road, Carshalton, SM5 1SL

Cost : £15.00 (Network will pay for candidates from South London Network clubs)

Disciplines : Field (Course Code SO/0192FD), Track (SO/0192TR) and Starter/Marksman (SO/0192SM)

Times : all courses are due to start at 10.30 am and finish at 3.30 pm

Acourse Application Form is also attached. There is no charge for SLAN member clubs. So there is no need to include the payment of £15.00 but please include which SLAN club you belong to on your application.
