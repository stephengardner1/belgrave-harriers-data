RWA 10m Champs., Warwick University, Coventry, 6 March 2010 .

Alex Wright wins RWA 10 miles

Alex Wright showed the benefit of his recent training stint in Australia and turned in a super performance to win the RWA National 10m Championship. He took the lead on the first of the thirteen laps and although closely followed by his rivals at first, pulled away from the field to secure a 1 min 48 sec. margin of victory in 70:46. Alex hopes to set a personal record over 20 kilometres in Lugano in a couple of weeks time.
