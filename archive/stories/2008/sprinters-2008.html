Sprinters - Do you want to earn some money?

Part-time Running Job

Purpose of the Job

We are looking to recruit a motivated team of people who are physically active, to be part of a high-profile sprinting project in an art gallery.

Main activities

You would be expected to run as fast as reasonably possible for a distance of approximately 90 m, with a walk back to the start point, setting off every 2 minutes during a ½ hour period. We are looking for a minimum commitment of 4 x ½ hr sessions per day with rest breaks in between. We would expect you to commit to at least 3 days a week (could include weekends) from 29 June – 16 November 2008. You would also be required to attend training sessions throughout June.

Selection

As part of a three step selection process, you would be required to attend an interview in late May, followed by a health screen and training session in late May.

For an application pack and further information, please email jobs@tate.org.uk
