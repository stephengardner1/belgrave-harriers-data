Loughborough International, Loughborough. Full results

Personal records all round

It was a good day for those tackling the barriers. The day after turning out for the Women's Southern League team, Jennifer Culley travelled to Loughborough and ran three seconds faster, just outside her best ever, to win over 400m hurdles again. Fellow Belgravian Michelle Carey placed second. Two personal bests came in the women's sprint hurdles where we had another one-two with Rachel King getting the better of Sarah Claxton and a further personal record came from Dominic Girdler in the men's hurdles. Racing for Scotland Kevin Nash ran his best time in years in the steeplechase, winning by six seconds in 8:50-odd.

Three Belgravians featured in the men's 5000m where David Anderson made a huge advance to win in 14:16.73 from Spencer Barden, back from training in Australia and the USA. Jonathan Blackledge ran to his second best time for the GB Junior team.

Scottish vests were to the fore as Scot Thompson placed 2nd in the shot and Graeme Oudney confirmed his Inter-Counties form by clocking another sub-1:53 time in the 800. The match 800 saw Inter-Counties victor Tim Bayley placing equal 2nd.

Brilliant discus throwing came from Shelley Newman who added 40 cm to her lifetime best set in 1998 but again she was beaten by Phillipa Roles of Wales. Goldie Sayers took the javelin and Ben Challenger went 2 cm higher than his Valencia performance to win the high jump.

Not to be left out, the sprinters were also getting in on the act with Tim Abeyie winning the invitation 100m in yet another personal best ever time.
