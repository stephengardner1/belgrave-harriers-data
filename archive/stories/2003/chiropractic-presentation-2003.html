Chiropractic Presentation

2003-12-08

Back to Health Wellness Centres Ltd, who provide Chiropractic care to a number of Belgrave Harriers, will be giving a free presentation on the benefits of Chiropractic care on Saturday December 6th at 4:00 pm at Belgrave Hall.

In August they presented to a number of Belgrave Harriers and are now offering those Belgrave Harriers who missed the first presentation a chance to see how they too can benefit from Chiropractic care.

Further information regarding Back to Health Wellness Centres Ltd can be found at Belgrave Hall, by visiting www.bthcc.co.uk or by contacting Roger Alsop.

If anybody is interested in attending this presentation please can they contact the Hon. Gen. Secretary to book their place.

Restored from the club's earlier website, belgraveharriers.com, and published here as originally written.
