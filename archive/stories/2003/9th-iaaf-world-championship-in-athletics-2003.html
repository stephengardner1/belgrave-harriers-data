9th IAAF World Championships in Athletics, Paris 2003

Belgrave Harriers have negotiated a package of deals for anybody wishing to attend the IAAF Track & Field World Championships in Paris in August. A key feature is that you can mix and match your travel arrangements as well as the number of days attendance. The offer is for five or ten day packages.

You may select stadium tickets for four evening events (depart 27 August) or nine evening events (depart 22 August). Your can also select two, three or four star hotels and self drive, Eurostar or flight tickets.

Prices range upwards from £349 inclusive.

For further information and a brochure contact Ian Dennis at Carrick Tours on 01926 511555 or eMail sports@carricktravel.com
