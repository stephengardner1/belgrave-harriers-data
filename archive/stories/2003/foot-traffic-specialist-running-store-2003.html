FOOT TRAFFIC

Paul Freary's specialist running store FOOT TRAFFIC is now open for business.

Paul tells us,

"Although I am located in Bolton, Lancashire, I will be able to offer mail order service on all items as well as very good discounts for all Belgrave Harriers.

The shop 'phone number is 01204 301230. Please give me a call for any of your running equipment requirements or email me on paul.freary@virgin.net or send a text message on 07736 836683 (this is a number dedicated to text enquires).

Hope to hear from you soon. Please tell all your friends!"

If you are in town visit FOOT TRAFFIC at:

463 Blackburn Road, Bolton BL1 8NN. Free parking behind the store, located on the A666, just 1 mile from the end of the M61-A666.
