South of Thames "Junior" Cross Country Race - Byfleet, 1913

In 1913 the South of Thames "Junior" Cross-Country races were held on a County basis. The joint Surrey and Berkshire event was decided at Byfleet and Belgrave Harriers won bronze team medals thanks to the efforts of the gentlemen pictured above. Left to right: R.W. Best (17th), J.Helm (9th), H. Parker (15th), E. Musselbrook (18th) and O. Horwood (16th). First team were Weybridge Athletic Club Harriers with 33 points, Epsom Harriers scored 48 points for 2nd and the Belgrave score tallied 66 points. It was the first time that the club had gained team medals in this event which was first held in 1910; a team win didn't come along until the 1925-26 season. Club colours were claret with gold hoops.
