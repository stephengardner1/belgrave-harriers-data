Training or Leisure Breaks in the beautiful Loire Valley (Ref BH/2)

"St Eloy" Lavardin, (Nr Vendome) France

The perfect Holiday Break in the beautiful Loire Valley where visitors are always made very welcome. There is so much to see and St. Eloy is the ideal centre for exploring this beautiful part of France.

Nearby are the famous Vineyards of Vouvray and Bourgeuil. There is walking, cycling and many other activities such as fly fishing, swimming and canoeing. As one would expect there are many French gastronomic delights, typical of the region. And Lavardin itself is truly a 'beautiful village' of France.

For more information on all the exciting things to do in this beautiful part of France, why not give Sue and Harry a ring? They will be delighted to help in any way they can!
