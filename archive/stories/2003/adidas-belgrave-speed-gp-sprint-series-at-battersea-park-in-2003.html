adidas Belgrave SPEED Grand Prix 2003 - Sprint Series

Sprint Series at Battersea Park

The regular sprint series that has been so popular for the last couple of seasons is back at Battersea for 2003. The top prize is £75 to the runner who gains the most points throughout the entire series. Points are awarded according to the IAAF Scoring Tables. Scores for 100 metres and 200 metres at each meeting will be totalled and the athlete with the highest total after all four meetings will be the winner.

Of course if you simply want to turn up for one or two events then you will be very welcome.

Aladies series will be held only if there is sufficient interest (40 or more women's sprint race entries throughout the four meetings) otherwise ladies can enter anyway and run in mixed or single sex races depending on numbers.

Entry fees: £2.50 per event before the day, £3.00 on the night. To enter the Endurance GP for the whole series before the first meet - this includes 100m and 200m - £15.00. All fees payable to Belgrave Sports Promotions Ltd.
