21-22

European Cup Super League, Florence, ITA. a=21, b= 22.

The opening day of the European Cup Super League saw five Belgravians in action as the temperature rose to 35°. Captain Mick Jones threw well up to form in the hammer but could manage no more than 8th and similarly positioned was high jumper Ben Challenger who equalled his season's best of 2.20m. Goldie Sayers improved in each round of the javelin, ending with 55.28m and 7th.

Afine run came from Andy Graffin in the 5000m where he set a personal best of 13:48.99 for fourth. Even in the last stages of the race Andy was still fighting for a place in the last three. New to the event, he later admitted later that he doesn't quite have the strength there just yet, although he would "rather be up there and 'die' than not be in contention." Amy Spencer ran second leg of the British women's sprint relay team.

Day two, and Neil Speaight came through a tactical 800m well to gain 4th while Tim Benjamin was fastest of the winning 4 x 400m quartet with a 45.3 clocking.
