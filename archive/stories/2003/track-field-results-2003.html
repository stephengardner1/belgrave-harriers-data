BMAF Champs, Derby. a=7th, b=8th.

Details from Roger Alsop:- Fine sprinting saw M55 middle-distance ace Charlie Dickinson win at both 1500m and 5000m. W35 Helen Alsop entered her first "Masters" and came away with a silver medal while M40 Ian Wells picked up a bronze in the sprint hurdles and W45 Jane Searle did likewise over 800m. Doug Fotheringham cleaned up in the walks - two more golds - but the most prolific medallists were Mike Small and Cliff Taylor. Mike collected no less than four golds in the M45 throws while M70 Cliff scored almost as heavily with a gold and three silvers.
