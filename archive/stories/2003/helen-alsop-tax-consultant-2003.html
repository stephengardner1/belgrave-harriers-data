Self Assessment/Personal Tax Returns

Income & Expenditure Accounts for Self Employed Individuals

With the 2002/03 tax year now over, it is time again to start gathering together the information required for the preparation of your tax return. Although the filing deadline is a long way off, it soon comes around. So, if you are self employed or are required to file tax returns, don't delay in bringing your tax affairs up to date.

Iam offering a personal and efficient service at a competitive rate. The cost for the preparation of your tax return, and accounts if required, may be around half the amount charged by your current adviser. As a guide, the service I am providing is particularly suited to the following people:-

Self employed individuals who need only prepare an income and expenditure statement

Employees who are required to file tax returns (generally those paying higher rate tax)

Individuals with rental income to report

Individuals who need to complete a repayment claim form to get a tax refund

Individuals with capital gains to report

If you would like further information, then call, or email, me on the above numbers. The initial consultation is free. So, don't put it off, deal with it now. Some key dates you should note are:-

31 July 2003 - second tax payment on account for 2002/03

30 September 2003 - submit return if you want the Inland Revenue to calculate tax/collect any further tax up to £2,000 through PAYE coding

5 October 2003 - where no tax return has been received, new sources of income or capital gains to be reported to Inland Revenue

31 January 2004 - deadline for submission of 2002/03 tax return and for payment of any tax due. First payment on account for 2003/04

Remember, if you miss deadlines or do not pay tax on time, you may be liable to interest and penalties.
