Friends of Belgrave

2003-12-18

Why not consider becoming a Friend of Belgrave Harriers.

We have recently opened this new category of membership to enable those who do not want to use our training facilities or take part in competition but who do want to know of forthcoming social functions and to maintain an association with our club.

Friends will receive our normal mail shots including fixture cards and our occasional club magazine and will also be entitled to buy adidas shoes and any other clothing that we are able to offer at regular Belgrave prices; this alone could easily cover the small Friends of Belgrave annual membership fee.

To become a Friend, simply e-mail us with your full name and address and we will send you an application form. Alternatively click here to download a membership form.

Friends of Belgrave Membership is just £10 per year, initially payable at the time of application and thereafter each 1st April.
