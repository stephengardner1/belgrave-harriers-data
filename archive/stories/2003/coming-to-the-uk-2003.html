Coming to the UK to compete?


Are you coming to the UK? Do you want to compete for Belgrave Harriers - one of the top clubs in the country? If so then you should send your details, including date of birth, to John Jeffery so that you can be registered with the British Athletics League and uk:athletics. You must be in the UK for two months before you will be allowed to compete for Belgrave Harriers but providing you know when you are arriving we can register you with the League before you arrive. However, this must be done by April 1st. There are no exceptions to this rule. A contact address in London would be handy as well as your intended date of arrival.

Restored from the club's earlier website, belgraveharriers.com, and published here as originally written.
