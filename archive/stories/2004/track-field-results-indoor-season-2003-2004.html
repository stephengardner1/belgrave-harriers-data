28-29

AAA U20 Champs. Birmingham.

It was a big weekend for our Juniors. James Ellington (18) took the U20 AAA 60m title in a startling 6.77 and followed up by breaking the club record for the 200m the next day. 17 year-old Set Osho twice reduced our U20 record for the 400m as he picked up a silver medal while Chris Gearing again heaved the shot out to a near record distance of 17.72 for yet another U20 win - and he's also only 17!

U20M. 60m: a 1 J.Ellington 6.79 (1s1 6.77); hts J.Regis 7.28. 200m: b 2 J.Ellington 21.59 (1h3 21.93, 2s1 21.45). 400m: b 2 S.Osho 48.68 (a 1s2 48.71); sf J.Hussain 51.34 (h 49.73). 800m: b 2 G.Oudney 1:55.83 (h 1:55.38). SP: b 1 C.Gearing 17.72; 3 K.Stevens 2-cl 16.75.

U17M. SP: 2 J.Thomas 15.57.

International Heptathlons. Mondeville, FRA.

AAA Champs. & World Champs Trial, Sheffield. Day Two.

Day two at the AAA Indoor Champs saw Sarah Claxton take the 60m hurdles from Rachel King in another club record (8.11). Records too for Caroline Stead (12.13m triple jump) and James Ellington (21.56 U20 200m). Tim Abeyie improved to his best ever performance to earn World Championship selection over 200m.

AAA Champs. & World Champs Trial, Sheffield. Day One.

Tracey Bloomfield-Grant set a superb club record of 4.15m to win the pole vault. Tracey was joined as a record holder by Sarah Claxton (6.31 long jump) and Michelle Carey (54.81 400m). Three Belgrave men took AAA Championships: Andy Graffin (8:06.59 3000m), Ben Challenger (2.20 high jump) and Julien Kapek (16.32 triple jump).
