Young Athletes Cross Country Results 2003-2004

2004-03-13

ESAA Champs, Mote Park, Maidstone, 13 March 2004.

In the Junior Boys (years 8 and 9) Tom Barry ( representing Dorset) had a blinder and worked through from 36th after 1500m to 24th at 3000m to finish 15th in a time of 17:43. The course was a hilly 4300m and a field of 320 finished.

1 G.Divall (West Midlands) 17:00; 2 P.Howarth (Beds) 17:12; 3. N.Bibby (Lancs) 17:18.
