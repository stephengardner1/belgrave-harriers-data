Swim training

2007-10-07

For a year now we've been organising swim training sessions at KCS Wimbledon on Thursday nights. These have now come to an end but we are now able to offer two sessions per week - Tuesdays and Saturdays - at an alternative venue.

Tuesday sessions are from 7pm to 8pm and Saturday sessions are from 9am to 10am. In each case the venue is Westcroft Leisure Centre, Westcroft Road, Carshalton, Surrey SM5 2TG. Sessions cost £5.

Please contact Lee Greatorex for further details.

Restored from the club's earlier website, belgraveharriers.com, and published here as originally written.
