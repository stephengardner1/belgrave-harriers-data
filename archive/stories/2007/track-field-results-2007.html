11th IAAF World Champs, Day Four, Osaka, Japan, 28 August 2007.

David Gillick close to his best

Only the first three in each heat of the 400m gained automatic qualification to the next round and some big names fell by the wayside.

David Gillick came up against out-and-out favourite Jeremy Wariner of USA in heat 2 but ran well to place 3rd, pushing GBR's Martin Rooney out of a qualifying spot in the process! Gillick's time of 45.35 compared well with his Irish Record of 45.23 set in Berne in May. Look out for that record going again come the next round.
