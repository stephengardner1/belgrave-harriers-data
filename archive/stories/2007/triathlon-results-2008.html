Triathlon Results 2008

2007-05-31

Trifest 08 Aquathlon, Emberton Country Park, Olney, Bucks., 31 May 2007.

750m swim 5k run

Last year at the British Age Group Aquathlon Champs Anne Hegvold had a tough day and failed to finish but she made no mistake in 2008, winning the W40-44 category to become British Age Group Champion.

1 - Anne Hegvold 10:19 22:39 - 32:59

London Triathlon League, Morden 6n6, 11 May 2007.

600m swim 6 mile run

Triathlon team manager Tim Weeks took bronze in this London Triathlon League event, thus equalling Lee Greatorex's position of 2007. The event consists of a 600 metre swim followed by a 6 mile run and for the first time Belgrave's Triathletes closed in a full team of five.

Talk about consistent! Versus her 2007 performance Anne Hegvold was one second faster in her swim and one second faster in her run - but dropped one place overall.
