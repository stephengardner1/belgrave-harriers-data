Women's Southern League 2001: Match 6, Cambridge

2001-09-01

What a result! And what a season! In fine style the Belles achieved their season’s aim, not only of promotion to Division 2, but going up as League Champions.

Abig thank you and congratulations to all the athletes, the officials, and supporters for making this possible. It’s been a long, hard slog, but we finally got there. Well done to Kim and Paul Collier for rounding up as many juniors as were available given the holiday season.

Even an operation the day before couldn’t keep Team Manager Cynthia James away from this momentous match. The girls showed their appreciation of all her hard work this season with champagne and chocolates.

We knew we would have a battle on our hands to win the league, so we were fortunate to be able to field such a strong team for this final match. All of our seniors placed in the top 3, with an amazing 25 first places. Personal bests still featured: Danielle Kandaros 400m 64.1; Catherine Eastham 3000m 12.17.7; Maureen Knight 100H 15.8; Michelle Nestor LJ 5.02.

If we’re going to succeed in Division Two next year, we’re going to need to discover some new junior talent, as again we suffered from a lack of athletes. The team of 5: Sharena McGregor, Shereena Miller, Louise Collier, Catherine Olawore and Emily Collins, again put in maximum effort to cover as many events as allowable. Both Sharon’s won their 100m, with a PB for Shereena Miller of 13.2. Other personal bests were for: Emily Collins 800m 2.53.8; Catherine Olawore SP 7.17 and JT 9.17 in her first attempt.

Well done Belles, and we look forward to seeing what next year brings…….
