Track & Field Men's Southern League 2001

2001-07-21

Match 5, Sutcliffe Park, 21 July 2001.

Just eight men plus Team Manager for the day Don Anderson covered match five at Sutcliffe Park and with such limited resources they did well to tackle 24 events between them and hang on to 4th team place. There have been days when we’ve done worse with a full squad!
