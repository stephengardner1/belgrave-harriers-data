AAA Championships 2001 - Belgrave H Results

2001-12-31

Discus thrower Shelley Drew: Belgrave's first ever female AAA Champion was also winner of the CAU Inter-Counties Champs. and winner of every round of the Jubilee Cup. She took the club record out to 59.10m whilst winning in the international match Great Britain & N.Ireland v France at Ashford on 11 August. Shelley married Belgravian Lee Newman on Saturday 15 September.
