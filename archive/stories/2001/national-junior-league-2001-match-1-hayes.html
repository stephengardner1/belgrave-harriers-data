National Junior League 2001: Match 1, Hayes

2001-04-29

At Hayes stadium, Belgrave's junior men, winners of the National Junior League title last season, had a tough time, finishing third to Enfield & Haringey and Shaftesbury Barnet. Lawrence Oboh completed an impressive 100m/200m double in 10.8/21.7 seconds and outstanding 15 year-old Gary Wilson long-jumped 6.24 metres to win the B event. Prolific racer Richard Ward won the 800 metres in 1:54 but his hard weekend (he had run at Birmingham in the AAA 12-Stage Road Relay the dat before) caught up with him in the 1500m where he had to settle for second in 4:05. 'The Simpsons' had the measure of the 400m by winning both A and B races in 48.9 seconds and 53.0 seconds respectively.
