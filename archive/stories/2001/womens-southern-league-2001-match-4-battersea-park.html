Women's Southern League 2001: Match 4, Battersea Park

2001-07-14

After a somewhat rainy start the skies cleared and the afternoon stayed dry. It was a day of many celebrations, some more hard fought than others. In addition to our celebrating 25 years of the Belgrave women’s team, Derek Jones and Rex Bale were also celebrating their silver anniversary with the club. That, coupled with a few birthdays, and another Belles win, led to a few glasses of wine and a giant cake being consumed at the end of the match.

We had the privilege of electronic timing for most of the day, although not without a few hitches. Sadly no club or stadium records were recorded.

Despite missing a few athletes due to the the AAA’s, the Belles managed to pull together and fight hard to maintain their unbroken record so far this season. There were even had a few personal bests.

In the juniors, Natasha Richardson clocked a pb of 13.8 in the 100m and 4.28m in the long jump, and in the seniors, Vicki Goodwin scored a pb in the 400m of 61.23. If I missed any other pb’s then very sorry, but some of you may have noticed I was rather distracted myself, competing in the pole vault and also managing a pb in the 3000m with 12.47.7.

We’re going to be very short of junior athletes for the next meeting at Swindon on the 11th August, so any girls not competing in the U17/U15 AAA’s that weekend please let Cynthia or Kim know if you can compete in the league match instead.
