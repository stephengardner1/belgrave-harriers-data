Jack Stirling-Wakeley

Also printed as A.J. STIRLING-WAKELEY, Jack

5 mentions

  • No. 110, page 15 — took part in Nijmegen Marches · printed page
  • September 1972, page 59 — died suddenly last December; won 24 hours track walk at Motspur Park 1948 with 122 miles 1,411 yards; won London to Brighton and Back in 18hrs. 46m.; Tommy Green Cup; Club Honours Badge; percussionist at the Royal Opera House; first Hon. Editor of the Road Runners Club Newsletter; represented Hampshire · printed page
  • March 1973, page 52 — won 24 hours race at Motspur Park 1948, Centurion No. 143 in 18:56:13, 122 miles 1411 yards; won Brighton and Back three years later in 18:46:0; death recently reported · printed page
  • April 1980, page 46 — Belgrave walker; founded the R.R.C. Newsletter in December 1952; won 24 Hours Track Walk at Motspur Park 1948 and London to Brighton and Back Walk 1951 · printed page
  • November 1995, page 62 · printed page
