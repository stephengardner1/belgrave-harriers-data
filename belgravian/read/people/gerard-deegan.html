Gerard Deegan

Also printed as Deegan, G.Deegan, Gerard, Gerard T. Deegan, Gerard's, Gerry Deegan

9 mentions

  • November 1975, page 8 — new member · printed page
  • August 1976, page 18 — represented Eire v Luxembourg and Wales; won 3000m steeplechase in 8:57.6, second fastest ever Belgravian · printed page
  • December 1976, page 5 — first claim Belgrave member running for Eire; 4th AAA 5,000m 13:41.2, beating club record by just over 3 seconds · printed page
  • December 1976, page 38 — 1976 club top twenty: 5,000 metres 13:41.2; 3,000 metres S'chase 8:57.6 · printed page
  • April 1977, page 54 — aged 21, won the Irish Cross Country Championships at Ennis by 20 seconds, 27 February 1977 · printed page
  • April 1977, page 59 — 2nd, 11 seconds behind the winner, in the six mile race at Fermoy on 6 February 1977; had beaten National Champion Brendan Foster by ten yards at Mallusk, Belfast · printed page
  • April 1977, page 40 — club record list: SENIOR MEN 5,000 Metres 13:41.2 · printed page
  • October 1977, page 48 — fastest ever Belgravian at 3,000m, 7th in 7:59.1 at Crystal Palace 18 May 1977; won 5,000m for Ireland v Greece at Limerick in 13:57.0 · printed page
