Bob Ricketts

Also printed as "Bob" Ricketts, Bob Ricketts', Mr. "Bob" Ricketts, “Bob” Ricketts

18 mentions

  • July 1929, page 12 — originated the Inter-Club Walking Contests; "whom we all hold in most affectionate remembrance" · printed page
  • April 1930, page 14 — inspired the 1922 team; as “B” team man beat Surrey Walking Club's fourth man in 1925 · printed page
  • April 1930, page 16 — then President; lost to the club; walked well over the last mile at Derby in 1925 · printed page
  • July 1930, page 8 — the late; originator of the Belgrave Inter-Club Walking Contest · printed page
  • July 1931, page 9 — the late; presented the "Doris" Shield · printed page
  • November 1936, page 3 — the late; tided the Club through several crises · printed page
  • November 1936, page 23 — recalled by Stan. A. Manning · printed page
  • October 1943, page 16 — worked for Vickers in the last war; trained at Erith Recreation Ground and found many members there · printed page
  • May 1944, page 3 — Club news typewritten publications in his days · printed page
  • July 1945, page 6 · printed page
  • December 1946, page 29 — pre-war Club Veterans' Walk · printed page
  • March 1948, page 7 — "one of our finest members"; subject of Jimmy Belchamber's portrait · printed page
  • December 1950, page 22 — late President; Bellchamber's attendant 1920; 8th 1921, 18th 1922; presented the Belgrave Cup in 1923 · printed page
  • February 1955, page 3 — the late; helped build the club's cash reserve · printed page
  • August 1963, page 5 — marriage announced in the October 1908 Club Circular · printed page
  • March 1967, page 4 — walker 1919-21 · printed page
  • June 1967, page 23 · printed page
  • October 1977, page 25 — seriously ill President, succeeded by Harry Hare · printed page
