Colin McLellan

Also printed as C McLellan

3 mentions

  • April 1925, page 5 — previous winner 1916 (48 29) · printed page
  • July 1925, page 5 — famous Scottish walking champion; joined Belgrave in the Great War with the Scots Guards; first member to win the Murphy Shield (6 mile road walking championship); won the 7 mile Championship of Scotland in Scottish record; records for each mile 1 to 4 in a 4 mile handicap · printed page
  • January 1928, page 10 — R. Murphy shield winner 1916 (6 miles), 48 29 · printed page
