John Clemens

Also printed as J. Clemens, J. W. Clemens, JOHN CLEMENS

5 mentions

  • September 1933, page 23 — 2nd Southern Championship; long jump 21ft. 4½in., 91.93% in Club Field Events Championship · printed page
  • June 1934, page 4 — relied on to score points in the Kinnaird Trophy · printed page
  • June 1934, page 23 — won the University of London long jump title; consistently over 21ft. 6in.; selected for the A.A.A. v. Oxford University (Long Jump) · printed page
  • July-August 1934, page 18 — gone to Manchester; selected as reserve for International competition at long jump; 3rd, "Harry Wright" Shield, Long Jump 20ft. 9¼in., 89.29% · printed page
  • September 1970, page 7 · printed page
