Fred Rickard

Also printed as F Rickard, F. Rickard

9 mentions

  • April 1925, page 4 — runner up in the club 10 mile walk championship the previous year · printed page
  • July 1925, page 5 — club 20 mile champion; retained National Police Champion title in the Barking-Southend Walk; first in the sealed handicap from scratch Belgrave member; National Police Champion — walked for the Police. · printed page
  • May 1926, page 2 — won 20 mile walk championship (R. W. Ricketts Shield), 2.47.49; holder; printed "F. Rickards" in the report text · printed page
  • June 1927, page 4 — holder for the three previous years; finished at 8 miles · printed page
  • June 1927, page 6 · printed page
  • November 1927, page 10 — 11th, "A" team · printed page
  • November 1927, page 17 — one of "our members" successful outside the Club; 7th in the All England Police 10 mile championship (a championship, not a club) and in the winning team (team not named) Belgrave member; competed in a Police championship. · printed page
  • May 1928, page 12 — 7th, Essex, 92m. 43s. · printed page
  • July 1975, page 26 — member of Belgrave's winning walking trio with Green and Bentley · printed page
