Letter · page 1

Belgrave Harriers.

W. H. HARE.

2, SUTHERLAND PLACE, S.W. 1.

April, 1925.

Dear Sir,

I am pleased to enclose a copy of the Club Gazette, which is hoped will become a regular publication.

The cost of this is admittedly heavy, 6d. per copy, but it is only by every members support that the actual cost be defrayed.

Will you kindly endeavour to assist by forwarding a remittance and advising me of your wish to continue to subscribe.

Thanking you in hopeful anticipation.

Yours faithfully,

W. H. HARE.

W. H. Hare
